<div class="section-header">
  <span class="section-tag">The problem</span>
  <h2>Remember the call, not just that you had one.</h2>
</div>

<p>Your call log records that the landlord called and talked for 14 minutes. What was actually <em>decided</em> — the date, the amount, the "I'll send it Friday" — lives only in your memory. Most AI note takers don't help here: they're built for scheduled meetings, with a calendar invite and a bot in the participant list. Arc's Call Insights is an AI note taker for real phone calls. You talk; Arc handles the stenography.</p>

---

<div class="section-header">
  <span class="section-tag">How it works</span>
  <h2>What happens on a call with Arc on.</h2>
</div>

<h3>During the call: live transcription with speaker ID</h3>

<p>With Call Insights turned on, the conversation is transcribed as it happens, each turn labeled by speaker — so six months later you can check whether "I'll send it Friday" was you or them:</p>

<pre>
Speaker 1: Hello, thanks for calling. How can I help you today?

Speaker 2: Hi, I wanted to discuss the project timeline.

Speaker 1: Sure, let me pull up the details. The current deadline is March 30th...
</pre>

<h3>When you hang up: the summary</h3>

<p>By the time you're back at your desk, the call insight report is ready, with the call's date, duration and source:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>A summary</strong> — what was discussed and decided. <em>"Agreed to extend deadline to April 5th due to additional feature requests. Client approved budget increase for extra work. Follow-up meeting scheduled for March 20th."</em></li>
  <li><strong>Action items</strong> — each promise as a task: update the timeline document, send the revised budget proposal by March 18th, schedule the follow-up for March 20th at 2:00 PM.</li>
  <li><strong>Key insights</strong> — facts worth remembering: budget approved for a 15% increase, the client prioritizes quality over speed, the next milestone is March 25th.</li>
</ul>

<div class="section-header">
  <span class="section-tag">Two modes</span>
  <h2>Two modes: Manual Upload and Auto Analysis.</h2>
</div>

<h3>Manual Upload — analyze a recording once</h3>

<p>Tap <strong>Upload Recording</strong> and pick a file — MP3, M4A, WAV, AAC, OGG or FLAC, up to 100 MB. Arc produces the same report: transcript, speakers, summary, action items, key insights. Good for dictaphone notes, a recorded interview, or the call you saved for a dispute.</p>

<h3>Auto Analysis — new recordings, handled</h3>

<p>If your phone already records calls (where it's legal), Auto Analysis watches a folder you choose through Android's document picker — Arc gets scoped access to <em>just that folder</em>. Set a file size limit (1–100 MB), a cutoff date so old recordings are ignored, and a check frequency of 6, 12 or 24 hours. New recordings are analyzed in the background and a notification opens the report directly. Failed files are retried up to three times, and Battery and Doze mode are respected.</p>

</div>

<p>Every analyzed call lands in your Call Insights list, newest first, grouped under <em>Today</em>, <em>Yesterday</em> and <em>This Week</em>, with a Manual/Auto badge and an unread dot. Search for <em>"deadline"</em> or <em>"March 15"</em> and Arc searches titles, transcripts, summaries and action items at once. <div class="section-header">
  <span class="section-tag">Privacy</span>
  <h2>Your calls, your device, your rules.</h2>
</div>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Explicit consent, always.</strong> Nothing is captured until you turn the feature on. Manual Upload means you picked the file; Auto Analysis means you granted one folder. There is no silent mode.</li>
  <li><strong>Scoped access only.</strong> Via Android's Storage Access Framework, Arc reads only the folder you granted, and you can revoke it any time.</li>
  <li><strong>Transcripts stay on your device.</strong> Transcripts, summaries and action items are stored locally. Audio goes to Google's AI service for transcription and isn't permanently stored there; Arc doesn't keep the audio file.</li>
  <li><strong>Optional encrypted backup.</strong> Include call insights in your Google Drive backup — off until you turn it on.</li>
  <li><strong>Banking and crypto apps: automatically off.</strong> Arc auto-disables itself in 200+ banking and crypto apps.</li>
</ul>

<p>Recording laws differ by country and state — some require everyone on the call to consent. Check the rules where you live.</p>
