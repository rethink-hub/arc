The quiet return of local-first software

Priya Raghunathan · 11 min read

For fifteen years the default answer was the server. Your documents lived somewhere else, your edits were round trips, and an aeroplane was enough to make the tool useless.

Local-first inverts that. The device holds the real copy; the network is a synchroniser, not a landlord. Conflict-free replicated data types made the merge tractable, and cheap local storage made the rest obvious.
