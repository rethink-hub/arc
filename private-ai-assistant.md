<div class="content-section">
  <h2>Three privacy decisions Arc was built on</h2>

  <h3>1. It's off in your banking apps — automatically</h3>
  <p>Arc carries a built-in list of 200+ banking, crypto, and payment apps — retail banks, exchanges, wallets — and disables itself the moment one of them is frontmost. There's nothing to configure and nothing to forget. It's a design constraint in the product, not a setting you can accidentally flip off.</p>

  <h3>2. It never monitors in the background</h3>
  <p>Arc reads the screen only when you invoke an action — a swipe of the sidebar on Android, the menu on macOS. No polling, no persistent capture. It's a tool you pick up, not a camera that's always rolling.</p>

  <h3>3. Screenshots require an explicit yes</h3>
  <p>The first time an action needs the screen as an image, Arc asks for consent before capturing anything. Decline, and text-based capture keeps working. You can also choose whether Arc asks for a region each time, always captures the full screen, or reuses your last selection.</p>

  <h3>Plus: you decide where Arc works</h3>
  <p>On top of the automatic banking list, a per-app allow/deny list lets you exclude anything else — a messaging app with sensitive threads, a password manager, a health app. The design is <strong>surgical, not blanket</strong>: fully functional where you allow it, absent where you don't.</p>
</div>

<div class="content-section">
  <h2>What happens on your device vs. what leaves it</h2>
  <p>Arc is not a fully offline AI, and pretending otherwise would be the fastest way to lose your trust. Here's the real breakdown:</p>

  <table class="comparison-table">
    <tr><th>What</th><th>Where it happens</th></tr>
    <tr><td>Language detection</td><td>On your device</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Saved library, flashcard decks, chat history, custom actions, Info Vault entries</td><td>On your device — local-first storage</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Reading the screen (accessibility layer)</td><td>On your device, on demand only</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Text-to-speech playback (AI Read)</td><td>On your device, using system voices</td></tr>
    <tr><td>AI generation — summaries, rewrites, chat, extraction</td><td>In the cloud, powered by Google Gemini</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Backup</td><td>Your own Google Drive, off by default, only if you connect it</td></tr>
    <tr><td>Crash and diagnostics reporting</td><td>Anonymous, optional, and fully off-able in Settings → Privacy</td></tr>
  </table>

  <p>AI generation needs the internet, and that request carries the content you asked it to act on. Everything else is local. Your library never uploads itself, and Info Vault entries travel only as context inside a generation <em>you</em> asked for.</p>
  <p>On macOS, Arc asks for two grants once: Accessibility (to read on-screen text) and Screen Recording (for screenshot actions, with the consent step above). On Windows, no special grants are needed.</p>
</div>

<div class="content-section">
  <h2>Your data belongs to you — and you can prove it</h2>
  <p><strong>Delete anything, any time.</strong> Saved items can be deleted inline or in bulk, with a counted confirmation so a mass delete never happens by accident. Chat sessions can be searched and bulk-deleted; Vault entries removed in one tap. There's no "contact support to erase your data" step, because none of it is locked up on our servers.</p>
  <p><strong>Backup is opt-in.</strong> Backup happens only through Google Drive, after you connect it in Settings → Backup — on demand or daily/weekly, disconnectable any time. Restoring is a merge, not an overwrite, so a restore can't destroy anything on the device. The trade-off: deletions don't propagate between devices.</p>
  <p><strong>Start as a guest.</strong> Use Arc with no account from the first launch. Sign in with Google only for cross-device sync, purchases, or backup.</p>
</div>

    <summary>Does Arc track what I do in other apps?</summary>
    <p>No. Arc reads the screen only at the moment you summon it — no background monitoring, polling, or persistent capture. Banking apps are excluded automatically, and you can exclude any other app with the per-app allow/deny list.</p>
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Where is my saved content stored, and can I delete it?</summary>

    <summary>How is this different from "no filter" AI apps?</summary>
