</div>

<h3>The three-face card: question, hint, answer</h3>

<p>Most flashcards are two-sided. Arc's have a middle face: see the question, try from memory, and if you're not there, press <strong>H</strong> for a hint — a nudge, not the answer — then flip. That moment of straining toward the answer is the difference between recognition ("oh yeah, I knew that") and recall, and recall is what survives to exam day. Know it cold? Skip the hint and flip straight through.</p>

<h3>Keyboard-first study mode</h3>

<p>On Mac and Windows the study loop never needs the mouse: <strong>Space</strong> flips, <strong>H</strong> shows the hint, <strong>← →</strong> page through, <strong>Esc</strong> closes. A counter pill shows <em>2 / 5</em>, and chevrons and click-to-flip are there for mouse users.</p>
