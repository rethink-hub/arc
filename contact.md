# Contact

Arc is made by one developer, so every message reaches the person who writes the code.

**Email:** [everythingrethink@gmail.com](mailto:everythingrethink@gmail.com)

**Response time:** up to 7 business days

## What to include

- **Bug reports:** your platform (Android, Mac or Windows), the app version, and what you did just before it happened. A screenshot helps.
- **Billing:** the email on your subscription and where you bought it (Google Play, the Mac app or the Microsoft Store).
- **Feature ideas:** what you were trying to do. That's more useful than the feature name.
