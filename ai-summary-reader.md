## Then Listen to It

Every summary has a play button. AI Read speaks it in a natural voice, skipping ads and formatting clutter, so a long newsletter or report becomes something you can hear while driving, cooking or exercising. Condensing first and listening second cuts the reading load for people with dyslexia, ADHD or low vision. For voices, speed and pitch settings and full-screen reading, see [AI text to speech]({{ '/text-to-speech/' | relative_url }}).

Content is processed to generate summaries and speech but never stored on Arc's servers.
