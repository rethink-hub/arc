Full transparency: I build [Arc](/), an AI screen assistant for Android. It's on this list because people use it and it wins at what it does — but I've tested each app below against the same standard: *does it actually save time every day?* Where a competitor beats Arc, I say so.

Here are the nine AI apps that earned a spot on my phone in 2026.

## 1. Arc — AI Screen Assistant (Best overall assistant)

**Free · Android + macOS**

The category Arc created: a floating AI sidebar that works on top of every app. Swipe from the screen edge and it appears over whatever you're doing — no switching, no copy-pasting.

**What it does:**
- Summarizes articles, PDFs, emails — with read-aloud for commutes
- Drafts replies and rewrites text using your saved personal info
- Extracts events, addresses, and OTP codes from any screen (tappable, saved before they expire)
- Transcribes and summarizes phone calls
- Custom AI actions for your repetitive routines

**Who it's for:** anyone who wants AI help without leaving their current app. Especially strong if you read a lot on your phone or handle lots of messages.

**Honest limit:** it's a screen assistant, not a full chatbot platform — for deep research sessions you might still open a dedicated chatbot.

[Get Arc free on Google Play →](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rethink.arc)

## 2. Google Gemini (Best built-in assistant)

**Free / AI Premium · Android + iOS**

Gemini replaced Google Assistant on modern Android and it's now genuinely good — especially with screen-awareness on Pixel and Galaxy. If you pay for AI Premium, it also lands in Gmail and Docs.

**Best for:** Google-native users. **Limit:** you leave your current app to use it.

## 3. Circle to Search (Best built-in feature)

Long-press the navigation bar, circle anything. It's the fastest "what is this?" tool on any phone — products, landmarks, homework. Ships with Pixel and Samsung flagships.

**Limit:** lookups only — no writing, summarizing, or automation.

## 4. ChatGPT (Best general chatbot)

**Free / $20 Plus · Android, iOS**

Still the deepest general-purpose chatbot. The Android app is solid, voice mode is excellent, and custom GPTs add range. The trade-off is the same as Gemini: it lives in its own app, isolated from what you're doing.

## 5. Sider (Best browser sidebar)

**Free / from $10/mo · iOS, Desktop, Web**

The original "AI sidebar" — a panel beside any webpage with GPT-4-class models. Great for web research and page summaries on desktop.

**Limit on Android:** reduced experience versus desktop.

## 6. Otter.ai (Best meeting transcription)

**Free / from $8.33/mo · Android, iOS, Web**

If you sit in meetings, Otter records, transcribes, and summarizes. Arc transcribes *phone* calls; Otter covers *scheduled meetings* — they complement each other.

## 7. Perplexity (Best for research)

**Free / $20/mo · Android, iOS**

Cited, up-to-date answers with a clean mobile app. When you need current facts with sources instead of a chat, Perplexity is the tool. Their Comet browser push makes it more agentic every month.

## 8. Notion AI (Best for note-takers)

**$10/mo add-on · Android, iOS, Web**

If your second brain lives in Notion, its AI can summarize, search, and draft inside your workspace. Pricing is per-member, which stings for solo users — but the workspace integration is real.

## 9. Tasker + AI plugins (Best for automation nerds)

**~$3 one-off · Android**

Not an AI app itself, but pair Tasker with an AI API and you can build automations Arc's custom actions don't cover. Highest effort-to-power ratio on this list.
